The Rules

How to Play

♠Setup

Each player gets 3 blind cards face-down, 3 table cards face-up, and 3 hand cards. Before the game starts, swap any hand card with a table card to build your best layout.

▶Taking a Turn

Play one or more cards of the same rank onto the pile. The card must be equal to or higher than the top card. After playing, draw back up to 3 hand cards while the deck lasts.

↓Can't Play?

If you have no valid plays, pick up the entire pile — it becomes part of your hand. The other player then takes their turn.

⬡Card Zones

Play from your hand first. Once empty, play from your table cards. When those are gone, flip blind cards one at a time. A blind card that can't be played means you take the pile plus that card.

🏆Winning

The first player to play their last card wins. The other player is crowned the Shizzman — and has no one to blame but themselves.

4×Four of a Kind

Playing four cards of the same rank on top of the pile burns it instantly — the cards are removed from play and you go again immediately.

Special Cards

Cards with superpowers.

Reset

Resets the pile — playable on anything. The next player can play any card they like.

7

Forces Low

Activates low mode — the next player must play a 7 or lower. Stacking 7s keeps low mode active.

10

Burns the Pile

Removes all cards in the pile from play. The same player goes again immediately.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026

Overview

Shizzman is a single-player offline card game. We take your privacy seriously — which is straightforward here, because the app collects no personal data whatsoever.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required to play.
  • No analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising SDKs are included in the app.
  • No usage data, crash reports, or diagnostics are sent to us or any third party.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other device data is accessed.

Local Data

Your settings (table colour, haptics preference, Dig and Pile Peek toggles) are stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

Shizzman does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

Shizzman does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The game contains no age-inappropriate content.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Given the nature of the app, we do not anticipate any material changes.
